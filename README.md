# XiaoLiuYa (Article Illustration Skill)

[ English ](README.md) | [ 简体中文 ](README_zh.md)

---

XiaoLiuYa is an AI-powered illustration skill designed for content creators and writers. By understanding article context and narrative mood, it automatically extracts visual metaphors and generates compelling, theme-matched illustrations and cover art to bring your writing to life.

Built upon the iconic visual language of the XiaoLiuYa emoticon, this skill transforms abstract concepts, workflows, state transitions, architectural boundaries, and narrative turning points into 16:9 widescreen, clean-white, highly restrained, hand-drawn explanatory illustrations with subtle humor and high readability.

## Core Philosophy and Aesthetic Principles

1. Explanatory Graphics Over Decorative Clipart
Illustrations exist to lower cognitive load and establish intuitive anchors for readers, not to decorate articles with irrelevant stickers. XiaoLiuYa must actively perform the central action or metaphor of the scene, never acting as a passive background bystander.

2. Rejecting Corporate Stiffness
Strictly avoid rigid corporate vector art, stiff PPT infographics, dense engineering blueprints, or dull black-and-white line art. The aesthetic simulates a thoughtful designer or architect casually sketching clear concepts on clean white paper using colored hand-drawn strokes.

3. Visual DNA Essentials
- Canvas Aspect Ratio: 16:9 widescreen format.
- Pure White Background: 100% clean white background with no gradients, drop shadows, paper textures, or heavy framing blocks.
- Character Authenticity: Rounded bean-shaped white body, thin charcoal/gray outlines, oversized round eyes with tiny black pupils, and a small flat orange-yellow beak positioned at the lower-middle of the face. Free of realistic anatomical duck features (no long necks, feathers, realistic wings, or duck tails).
- Palette Harmony: Faithful to the original emoticon palette. White body with orange-yellow beak and feet, complemented by low-saturation pastel tones (pale yellow, mint green, soft sky blue, gentle pink, and warm orange).
- Generous Whitespace and Minimal Annotations: Main subjects occupy 40% to 60% of the canvas, preserving at least 35% empty whitespace. Handwritten annotations are kept strictly minimal (3 to 6 short phrases), and artificial category banners (such as "Workflow Diagram" or "System Architecture") are strictly prohibited.

## Composition Patterns and Metaphors

The skill provides 8 core structural archetypes to adapt to various narrative requirements:

1. Workflow Pipeline: Visualizing input-to-output pipelines where XiaoLiuYa operates key transition checkpoints.
2. Local Subsystem: Highlighting central hubs coordinating with connected appliances, tools, or microservices.
3. Before vs After: Contrasting cumbersome legacy patterns with streamlined modern alternatives.
4. Character State: Portraying emotional and operational transitions across different project phases.
5. Conceptual Metaphor: Grounding abstract technical concepts into familiar physical items (e.g., safe deposit boxes for data sovereignty).
6. Layered Architecture: Demonstrating dependencies through hand-drawn stacked blocks or building bricks.
7. Roadmap and Wayfinding: Showcasing branching paths, decision junctions, and cautionary detour signs.
8. Mini Comic Strip: A 2-to-3 panel strip resolving typical conflict scenarios with humor and clarity.

## End-to-End Workflow

XiaoLiuYa supports a two-phase workflow:

### Phase 1: Context Analysis and Shot List Strategy
When given an article with a request to plan or analyze illustrations, the agent reads the context and provides a structured shot list containing:
- Recommended placement paragraph
- Illustration theme
- Core takeaway
- Selected composition archetype
- XiaoLiuYa action and context
- Visual elements
- Handwritten annotation phrases

### Phase 2: Single-Image Generation and QA
When explicitly commanded to generate images:
1. Reference Alignment: The agent inspects `assets/xiaoliuya-reference/contact-sheet.jpg` to anchor character proportions and facial geometry.
2. Prompt Synthesis: Assembles a structured prompt following `references/prompt-template.md` (16:9, clean white background, character traits, metaphor, and minimal annotations).
3. Tool Invocation: Calls the underlying image generation engine once per image.
4. QA Audit and Editing: Evaluates output against `references/qa-checklist.md`. If unnecessary category titles appear, an automated inpainting edit removes the title and restores pure white background.
5. Delivery: Copies validated images to `assets/` and provides a concise delivery summary.

## Quality Assurance Checklist

Before finalizing any illustration, verify the following:
- Character Fidelity: Are the oversized round eyes, flat lower beak, and rounded bean shape preserved without realistic animal anatomy?
- Central Agency: Is XiaoLiuYa actively engaged in the core concept rather than placed as decorative background?
- Canvas Cleanliness: Is the background 100% pure white with at least 35% empty space?
- Aesthetic Restraint: Are corporate vector styles, dense PPT charts, and heavy black-and-white hatching avoided?
- Minimal Text: Are annotations short, organic, and free of artificial category banners?
